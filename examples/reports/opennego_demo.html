OpenNegotiation Overview

A market lifecycle view of negotiation: agents do not just chat, they gather evidence, make structured offers, and pass accepted deals through transaction validation.

Artifact-backed demo
Buyer AgentBudget, preferences, search tools, and offer policy.
Market EvidenceSold comps, provenance, noisy/stale/failure regimes.
Negotiation ProtocolMessages, offers, accepts, rejects, and private tool traces.
Transaction LayerShipping, tax, fees, escrow, payment, and risk outcomes.

The public benchmark reads versioned artifacts: scenario, manifest, trace JSONL, and metrics.

Why Choose OpenNegotiation

Agentic, not prompt-only

Agents can use tools for market search, provenance checks, and transaction reasoning before making offers.

Market-grounded

Scenarios are anchored to comparable sale distributions instead of assuming a hidden human cost price.

Transaction-aware

Accepted offers can be settled through shipping, tax, fees, escrow, payment, and risk simulators.

Mechanism

1. ScenarioProduct cluster, condition, budget, private reservation, market reference.
2. ToolsPrivate or shared market-comparable search with provenance.
3. NegotiationStructured messages, offers, accepts, rejects, and tool calls.
4. SettlementShipping, tax, fees, escrow, payment, and risk outcomes.
5. MetricsDeal rate, market gap, utility, violations, and tool behavior.
6. ReplayVersioned artifacts support audit, visualization, and leaderboard views.

Key Findings From This Smoke

Tools change price formation

The buyer-private market tool pulls offers toward the sold-comparable reference instead of the inflated asking price.

Noisy evidence remains visible

Tool regime metadata and trace provenance make clean, noisy, stale, adversarial, and failure cases auditable.

Transactions are part of eval

The accepted offer is not the final story: buyer total cost, seller net revenue, fees, and risk are separately scored.
