OpenNegotiation: A Real-Market Negotiation Gym for AI Agents

OpenNegotiation is an evaluation and improvement framework for agent-to-agent negotiation. It moves negotiation benchmarks beyond static prompt roleplay by giving agents market evidence, tool access, private constraints, transaction rules, and traceable outcomes.

What OpenNegotiation Does

OpenNegotiation turns each negotiation into a reproducible environment: a market-grounded scenario, two agents with role-specific constraints, optional tools, structured negotiation actions, transaction settlement, and versioned artifacts for evaluation or training.

benchmark + training gym

Benchmark models

Compare buyer and seller agents on deal quality, market gap, utility, constraint violations, and transaction validity.

Evaluate tool use

Measure whether agents search at the right time, inspect provenance, resist stale comps, and convert evidence into offers.

Improve policies

Use NPC sparring and battle reports to extract strategy-layer prompt patches from failures.

Why This Matters

Real negotiation agents will not rely only on model priors. They will search market prices, compare recent transactions, reason about fees and shipping, probe counterpart constraints, and decide when a deal is worth closing. A realistic benchmark needs to evaluate that whole loop.

Real market grounding

Scenarios are anchored to comparable-sale distributions and all-in price assumptions instead of hidden toy cost variables.

Agentic behavior

The environment records tool calls, private evidence, structured offers, accept/reject actions, and negotiation traces.

Reusable opponents

Preset NPC strategies stress specific failure modes such as high anchors, stale evidence, hidden fees, and deadline pressure.

Mechanism

OpenNegotiation evaluates the full path from market setup to negotiated settlement, then turns the resulting artifacts into benchmark scores and training feedback.
