OpenNego: Real-Market Negotiation Gym for AI Agents

Benchmark, spar, and improve agent-to-agent negotiation policies against market-grounded scenarios, tool-aware opponents, and auditable deal traces.

Benchmark + training + strategy marketplace

From Benchmark to Policy Learning

OpenNego turns each negotiation into inspectable artifacts: market evidence, structured offers, private tool calls, deal outcomes, and strategy-layer recommendations.

Fixed base protocol + editable strategy
Real-Market BenchmarkScenario clusters are anchored to comparable sale distributions, not hidden toy costs.
Tool-Aware RunsAgents can search, cite, overtrust, or ignore evidence before making offers.
NPC SparringPreset sellers and buyers test anchors, stale comps, hidden fees, probing, and time pressure.
Policy ExtractionBattle reports convert failures into strategy prompt patches while the sandbox base stays fixed.

What OpenNego Makes Measurable

Market gap

Measure whether deals land near real comparable-sale references, including overpay and underpricing failure modes.

Tool discipline

Audit when agents search, cite provenance, react to stale evidence, and convert evidence into concrete offers.

Strategy learning

Extract trace-grounded lessons and reusable strategy patches from losses against tougher NPC profiles.

Mechanism

1. ScenarioProduct cluster, condition, budget, private reservation, market reference.
2. ToolsPrivate or shared market-comparable search with provenance.
3. NegotiationStructured messages, offers, accepts, rejects, and tool calls.
4. SettlementShipping, tax, fees, escrow, payment, and risk outcomes.
5. MetricsDeal rate, market gap, utility, violations, and tool behavior.
6. ReplayVersioned artifacts support audit, visualization, and leaderboard views.
