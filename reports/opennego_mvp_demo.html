OpenNego Overview

A market lifecycle view of negotiation: agents do not just chat, they gather evidence, make structured offers, and pass accepted deals through transaction validation.

Artifact-backed demo
Buyer AgentBudget, preferences, search tools, and offer policy.
Market EvidenceSold comps, provenance, noisy/stale/failure regimes.
Policy LearningBattle reports extract trace-grounded lessons and strategy prompt patches.
Strategy MarketplacePreset NPC profiles expose anchors, stale evidence, hidden fees, and probing tactics.

The MVP loop is benchmark, diagnose, patch strategy, and spar against harder NPC profiles.

Why Choose OpenNego

Agentic, not prompt-only

Agents can use tools for market search, provenance checks, and transaction reasoning before making offers.

Training-ready

Every run can become a battle report with failure diagnoses and strategy-layer prompt patches.

Marketplace-driven

Curated NPC strategies give users repeatable sparring partners for systematic optimization.

Mechanism

1. ScenarioProduct cluster, condition, budget, private reservation, market reference.
2. ToolsPrivate or shared market-comparable search with provenance.
3. NegotiationStructured messages, offers, accepts, rejects, and tool calls.
4. SettlementShipping, tax, fees, escrow, payment, and risk outcomes.
5. MetricsDeal rate, market gap, utility, violations, and tool behavior.
6. ReplayVersioned artifacts support audit, visualization, and leaderboard views.

Strategy Marketplace

Preset NPC profiles provide repeatable sparring partners. Each profile separates fixed sandbox base logic from editable strategy behavior.

5 NPC profiles

Hardline Anchor Seller

seller medium

Anchors near listing price, concedes slowly, and tests whether buyers can stay disciplined against high opening offers.

Tests: High opening anchor can pull weak buyers above market reference.; Slow concessions can force timeout if the buyer lacks close-mode policy.

Tags: seller, anchor, concession, baseline

Stale Comps Seller

seller hard

Uses stale or selective market evidence to defend price and tests whether buyers verify provenance before accepting.

Tests: Stale market evidence can mislead agents that do not inspect provenance.; Selective comparable sales can make market gap look smaller than it is.

Tags: seller, market-evidence, stale, tool-aware

Hidden Fees Seller

seller hard

Keeps negotiated price attractive while shifting attention away from shipping, escrow, payment, and platform costs.

Tests: Low item price can hide high all-in transaction cost.; Late concessions can cause buyers to accept before computing settlement risk.

Tags: seller, transaction, fees, risk

Budget Probe Buyer

buyer medium

Uses low anchors and repeated justification requests to discover weak seller reservation logic.

Tests: Low anchors can push sellers below market if they lack price floors.; Repeated justification requests can expose reservation logic.

Tags: buyer, probe, reservation, baseline

Tool Overuse Buyer

buyer easy

Calls tools repeatedly but struggles to convert evidence into concrete offers, useful for testing tool-use discipline.

Tests: Excessive search can burn negotiation turns.; Evidence collection without offer policy causes timeout.

Tags: buyer, tool-use, timeout, training
