Scoring Tracks

The final benchmark should rank models across role-specific and tool-aware tracks instead of treating one buyer smoke run as the whole story.

Buyer Track

Price discipline, market evidence use, close timing, and constraint compliance under seller pressure.

Seller Track

Reservation discipline, truthful evidence handling, concession policy, and transaction-risk disclosure.

Tool-Aware Track

Search timing, provenance inspection, stale-comparison resistance, and evidence-to-offer conversion.

Scorecard Contract

OpenNegotiation ranking should combine deal quality with behavior-level signals so agents cannot win by simply overpaying, stalling, or ignoring transaction risk.
