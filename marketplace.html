Strategy Contract

Marketplace profiles are prompt-level strategies around a fixed sandbox base. This keeps comparisons stable while allowing users to optimize their agent policy.

Fixed Base

Environment rules, action schema, private tool trace handling, settlement checks, and metric definitions remain fixed by OpenNegotiation.

base: protocol: opennegotiation.negotiation.v1 actions: [message, offer, accept, reject, tool_call] traces: versioned and auditable user_editable: false

Editable Strategy

Role behavior, concession policy, evidence policy, and close-mode tactics are editable strategy-layer instructions.

strategy: role: seller_or_buyer objective: stress a concrete failure mode traps: market, tool, transaction, timing user_editable: true
